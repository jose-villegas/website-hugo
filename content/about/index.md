Hi, I'm José Villegas, a game programmer with a computer science degree specialized in computer graphics. I'm from Caracas, Venezuela, and live in Berlin. I was making games before I graduated, so moving from graphics into game development felt natural.

Games let us build worlds with their own rules: you can't double jump in real life, but you can in a game. Making that work is a real engineering challenge, and almost always a team effort, so I keep learning from the artists, designers and musicians I work with.

My CV is on [GitHub](https://github.com/jose-villegas/cv-english/blob/master/main.pdf).
